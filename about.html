ABOUT CAMPUSHUB

Making Campus Issues
easier to report and resolve.

CampusHub is a centralized platform designed to help students report problems around campus and stay informed about their progress from submission to resolution.

Report an Issue View Issues
WHAT IS CAMPUSHUB?

One place for campus concerns.

CampusHub provides students with a straightforward way to bring campus problems to the attention of the people responsible for resolving them.

Instead of relying on scattered messages or informal complaints, students can submit an issue with useful details such as its category, location, description, and supporting information.

From report to resolution

Every complaint can be tracked through its status so students can understand what is happening with an issue after it has been reported.

HOW IT WORKS

A simple reporting process.

CampusHub connects student reports with an organized resolution workflow.

01

Report

Students submit a complaint and provide the relevant details about the problem and its location.

02

Review

The complaint can be reviewed by the campus administration and given an appropriate status.

03

Schedule

If action is required, administrators can provide a scheduled date, time, and approximate fixing duration.

04

Resolve

Once the issue has been addressed, its status can be updated to fixed or resolved.

ISSUE STATUS

Know where a complaint stands.

Pending

The complaint has been submitted and is waiting for review.

Under Review

The complaint is being examined by the administration.

Scheduled

Action has been planned for a specified date or time.

In Progress

Work related to resolving the issue is underway.

Fixed / Resolved

The reported issue has been addressed.
